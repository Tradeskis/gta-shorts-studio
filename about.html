About & contact

Highlight Reel Studio is run by David Knapp. It is the tool behind our own gaming clip channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

Highlight Reel Studio is an independent tool. It is not affiliated with, sponsored or endorsed by TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, or any game publisher, including Rockstar Games and Take-Two Interactive. Game names and footage shown in our videos belong to their owners.

The Studio is used by our team to run our channels. Team members log in with the TikTok account of the channel they manage.

Contact

Email dknapptravels@gmail.com. We answer within two working days.

Creators: if a clip of your stream is on one of our channels and you would like it credited differently or removed, email us with the link and we will act on it promptly.

Questions

Where does the footage come from?
Our own gameplay, creators who run open clipping programs or gave us permission, and publishers' official trailers and artwork. Every clip credits its creator.
Does anything post without a person checking it?
No. Every Short is reviewed before it is booked to post.
What does the Studio do with TikTok, YouTube or Instagram data?
See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is read, stored and for how long.
How do I log in or out?
Use Log in at the top of every page. Log out ends your session; Disconnect TikTok on the account page revokes the Studio's access to your TikTok account.
