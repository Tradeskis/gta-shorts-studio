About & contact

GTA Shorts Studio is run by David Knapp. It is the in-house tool behind our GTA gameplay channels, among them GTA 6 Secrets, GTA 6 NPC Moments, GTA 6 Lore, GTA 6 Chaos and GTA 6 Viral Moments, on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok.

The Studio is used by our own small team to run our own channels. It does not offer sign-ups, and it never posts to anyone else's accounts.

Contact

Email ddresler20@gmail.com. We answer within two working days.

Creators: if a clip of your stream is on one of our channels and you would like it credited differently or removed, email us with the link and we will act on it promptly.

Questions

Where does the footage come from?
Our own gameplay, creators who run open clipping programs or gave us permission, and Rockstar Games' official material. Every clip credits its creator.
Does anything post without a person checking it?
No. Every Short is reviewed before it is booked to post.
What does the Studio do with TikTok, YouTube or Instagram data?
It only connects to our own channels' accounts. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what is read and stored.
Can I use the Studio for my own channel?
Not at the moment; it runs our own channels only.
