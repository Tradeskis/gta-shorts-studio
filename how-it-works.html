How it works

From a stream to a post, and exactly what the Studio does with each platform account.

  1. 1

    Connect an account

    On a channel's page, press Connect with TikTok (or YouTube, or Instagram). The platform's own sign-in page opens; we sign in to the channel's account and approve the permissions. The Studio then shows which account is connected.

  2. 2

    Clip and review

    The Studio clips a stream into candidate Shorts. Each one waits in Review, where a person watches it and approves or rejects it.

  3. 3

    Choose how it posts

    Before a TikTok post the Studio reads the account's current settings and shows them: the account name, who can see the post (chosen by us, never pre-selected), whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed (off until ticked), and the commercial content disclosure for branded or paid content.

  4. 4

    Post now or on schedule

    The Short is posted straight away or at the channel's next posting slot. The post's link appears on the Posting dashboard as soon as the platform confirms it.

  5. 5

    Track performance

    The Studio reads the view, like, comment and share counts of the videos it posted on our own accounts, and shows them per channel and per platform.

TikTok permissions we use

user.info.basic

Shows which TikTok account is connected to which channel, by name and avatar.

video.upload and video.publish

Sends an approved Short to that account, as a draft or as a direct post with the settings chosen in step 3.

video.list

Reads the view, like, comment and share counts of the account's own videos for the Posting dashboard.

Access can be removed at any time with Disconnect in the Studio, or in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services.
