How it works

From a stream to a post, and exactly what the Studio does with each platform account.

  1. 1

    Log in with TikTok

    Press Log in (or Connect with TikTok on a channel's page in the Studio). TikTok's own sign-in page opens; you sign in to the channel's account and approve the permissions. The Studio then shows which account is connected. Log out revokes the access at TikTok.

  2. 2

    Clip and review

    The Studio clips a stream into candidate Shorts. Each one waits in Review, where a person watches it and approves or rejects it.

  3. 3

    Choose how it posts

    Before a TikTok post the Studio reads the account's current settings and shows them: the account name, who can see the post (chosen by the user, never pre-selected), whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed (off until ticked), and the commercial content disclosure for branded or paid content.

  4. 4

    Post now or on schedule

    The Short is posted straight away or at the channel's next posting slot. The post's link appears on the Posting dashboard as soon as the platform confirms it.

  5. 5

    Track performance

    The Studio reads the view, like, comment and share counts of the videos it posted on the connected account, and shows them per channel and per platform.

TikTok permissions we use

user.info.basic

Shows which TikTok account is connected, by display name and profile picture.

video.upload and video.publish

Sends an approved Short to that account, as a draft or as a direct post with the settings chosen in step 3.

video.list

Reads the view, like, comment and share counts of the account's own videos for the Posting dashboard.

Access can be removed at any time with Log out or Disconnect, or in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services.
