Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 24 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how GTA Shorts Studio ("the Studio", "we", "us") collects, uses, stores, shares and deletes information when you visit this website, log in with TikTok, or use the Studio to connect a TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account. By using the website or the Studio you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use them.

1. Who we are

GTA Shorts Studio is operated by David Knapp, an individual based in the United States, who is the data controller for the information described here. Contact: dknapptravels@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

a. When you log in with TikTok. With your permission, TikTok gives us, under the scopes you approve:

  • user.info.basic: your TikTok open ID (an identifier unique to our app), display name and profile picture (avatar URL).
  • video.list (only if you approve it in the Studio): the list of your public videos with their IDs, links and view, like, comment and share counts.
  • video.upload and video.publish (only if you approve them in the Studio): permission to send videos you choose to your account. We receive the publish status and the resulting post ID.
  • An access token and a refresh token that let the Studio act on your behalf within those scopes.

We never receive your TikTok password, phone number, email address, messages, followers list, or any data about other TikTok users.

b. When you connect YouTube or Instagram in the Studio. Through Google OAuth (youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly) and Meta's Instagram API: the channel or account name and ID, access tokens, and statistics of the videos posted through the Studio (views, likes, comments, shares, retention).

c. Content you create. Video files, transcripts of their audio, captions, titles and hashtags made in the Studio.

d. Website visits. This website uses no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies. The log-in page sets two strictly necessary, HttpOnly cookies: a one-time security code during sign-in (deleted after 10 minutes) and an encrypted session cookie holding your access token (deleted when you log out, or after 24 hours). Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare) may process your IP address and browser details in their server logs to deliver and protect the site.

3. How we use information

  • To show which TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account is connected (display name and picture).
  • To publish the videos you approve to the account you choose, with the privacy level, interaction settings and disclosure you select for each post.
  • To show the performance of videos posted through the Studio on its dashboard.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent misuse and fix errors.
  • To answer your messages and handle removal requests.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell or rent it, we do not build profiles of you, and we do not use TikTok data to train artificial-intelligence models.

4. Legal bases (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where these laws apply, we process your information to perform our service at your request (contract), on the basis of your consent given on TikTok's, Google's or Meta's permission screen (which you can withdraw at any time), and for our legitimate interests in keeping the service secure.

5. How we share information

We share information only as follows:

  • TikTok, Google (YouTube) and Meta (Instagram): the videos and captions you choose to publish are sent to the platform you choose, under that platform's own privacy policy.
  • Service providers that process data for us under their terms: Cloudflare (log-in page hosting), GitHub (website hosting), Buffer (scheduling some posts), and Anthropic (short transcript excerpts and a few low-resolution video frames, to judge clips and write captions; never your TikTok account data).
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, a court order, or to protect the rights, safety or property of anyone.

We never sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

6. Storage and security

Access tokens are encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest: on the website, in an AES-GCM encrypted HttpOnly cookie; in the Studio, in the operating system's credential store or an encrypted file on our own computers. Client secrets stay on the server and never reach your browser. Access is limited to the operator. No method of storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information and will notify affected users of a breach as the law requires.

7. How long we keep information

  • Website session cookie: until you log out, or 24 hours at most.
  • Studio access tokens: until you disconnect the account or revoke access; TikTok refresh tokens expire after 365 days.
  • Video statistics and post links: for as long as the post is live, and deleted within 30 days after you ask.
  • Working video files: deleted automatically after 14 days.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Revoke access at any time: Disconnect TikTok on this website's account page, Disconnect in the Studio, or in TikTok under Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services (Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions; Meta: Settings › Apps and websites).
  • Access, correction, deletion and portability: email dknapptravels@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days (45 days where California law allows). We may need to verify that the request comes from the account holder.
  • California residents (CCPA/CPRA): you have the right to know what personal information we collect, use and disclose, to delete it, to correct it, and not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use or disclose sensitive personal information.
  • EEA/UK residents (GDPR): you also have the right to object to or restrict processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. Children

The website and the Studio are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we will delete it.

10. International transfers

We are based in the United States and our providers may process data in other countries. Where required, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

11. Third-party services

Your use of TikTok, YouTube, Google and Instagram is governed by their own terms and privacy policies, which we encourage you to read: TikTok Privacy Policy (tiktok.com/legal/privacy-policy), Google Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and Meta Privacy Policy (facebook.com/privacy/policy). The Studio's use of YouTube data follows the YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

12. Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page with a new date above. If a change is material, we will say so on the log-in page before it takes effect.

13. Contact

Questions, requests or complaints: dknapptravels@gmail.com.
