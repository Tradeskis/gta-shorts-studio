GTA Shorts Studio

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

What this is

This policy covers GTA Shorts Studio, a private, single-operator desktop tool that edits gameplay footage into short vertical videos and publishes them to the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

The tool is not distributed, not offered as a service, and has no users other than its owner. It runs on the owner's own computer.

Who operates it

David Knapp. Contact: ddresler20@gmail.com

What data is accessed

The tool signs in only to accounts its owner controls:

  • YouTube (Google OAuth): youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, yt-analytics.readonly, to upload videos, check processing and read view counts of the owner's own videos.
  • TikTok (Login Kit): user.info.basic to show which account is connected, and video.upload to send a video to that account's TikTok inbox as a draft, which the owner finishes and posts in the TikTok app.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to the owner's own Instagram accounts.

No data belonging to any other person or account is accessed.

What data is stored, and where

Sign-in tokens are stored on the owner's own computer, protected by the operating system's credential store or an encrypted file. The tool also stores locally the owner's video files, transcripts, generated titles and captions, and view counts for the owner's own videos. None of it is sent to any server operated by the author, because no such server exists.

What data is shared

Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising. In normal operation:

  • Google / YouTube, TikTok and Meta / Instagram receive the videos the owner publishes and their titles, captions and tags, under their own privacy policies.
  • Anthropic receives short transcript excerpts and a few low-resolution frames to judge which moments to publish and to write titles, under Anthropic's privacy policy.

No personal information about any third party is sent to any of them.

Cookies and tracking

The tool has no web interface available to anyone else, sets no cookies, and performs no tracking or analytics on any person.

Data retention and removal

Working files are deleted automatically after fourteen days. Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account in the tool or revokes access at Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions), TikTok (Settings › Security › Apps and services) or Meta.

Children

The tool is operated by an adult and is not directed at children.
