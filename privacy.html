Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

What this covers

This policy covers GTA Shorts Studio, the editing and publishing tool that runs our own GTA gameplay channels on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok. The Studio runs on our own computers and connects only to accounts we control.

Who we are

GTA Shorts Studio is operated by David Knapp. Contact: dknapptravels@gmail.com.

What data is accessed

  • TikTok (Login Kit and Content Posting API): user.info.basic to show which account is connected; video.upload and video.publish to send our approved videos to that account as a draft or a post, with the privacy level, interaction settings and disclosure we choose; video.list to read view, like, comment and share counts of that account's own videos.
  • YouTube (Google OAuth): youtube.upload, youtube.readonly and yt-analytics.readonly, to upload our videos, check their processing and read their statistics.
  • Instagram (Meta): permission to publish Reels to our own Instagram accounts and read their insights.

No data belonging to any other person or account is accessed.

What data is stored, and where

Sign-in tokens are stored on our own computers, in the operating system's credential store or an encrypted file. The Studio also stores our video files, transcripts, captions and the statistics of our own posts. None of it is sold or published.

What data is shared

Nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising. In normal operation:

  • TikTok, Google / YouTube and Meta / Instagram receive the videos we publish and their captions, under their own privacy policies.
  • Buffer, a scheduling service, receives some of our videos and captions when a post is scheduled through it.
  • Anthropic receives short transcript excerpts and a few low-resolution frames to judge moments and write captions.

No personal information about viewers or other users is collected or sent to anyone.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or tracking.

Retention and removal

Working files are deleted automatically after fourteen days. Tokens are kept until the account is disconnected in the Studio or access is revoked at TikTok (Settings and privacy › Security › Apps and services), Google (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or Meta. To ask about or remove any data, email dknapptravels@gmail.com.

Children

The Studio is operated by adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

Any change to this policy is published on this page with the date above updated.
