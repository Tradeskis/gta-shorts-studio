Long GTA streams in, finished Shorts out.

GTA Shorts Studio is the editing and publishing studio behind our GTA gameplay channels. It finds the best moments in long streams, cuts them into vertical videos with captions, and posts them to our own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts, with a person approving every clip.

See how it worksFeatures

What the Studio does

One place to go from a three-hour stream to a week of short videos, without losing track of what went where.

1

Finds the moments

Reads a stream's transcript, chat and keyframes to find the funniest and most surprising stretches, and suggests where each clip should start and end.

2

Edits them for vertical

Puts the streamer's webcam above the gameplay, adds readable captions and a short hook, and keeps every graphic inside each app's safe area.

3

Publishes on schedule

Each approved Short is booked into a posting slot and goes to the matching channel on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok, the same clip at the same time.

Built around review

Nothing posts until a person has watched it. The Studio rates, ranks and prepares clips; we decide which ones go out.

Review queue

Watch each Short, approve or reject it with a reason, fix the hook or the trim, and the Studio learns from every decision.

Posting dashboard

One view per platform shows every channel's last and next 24 hours: what posted, what is booked, what failed and how many views each post has.

Credit and permission

Every clip records the creator it came from and their clipping permission, and the creator is credited in the caption.
