Features

Everything the Studio does, from a long recording to a post on each platform.

Finding clips

Stream scanning

Long streams and VODs are scanned for their hottest stretches using the clips viewers made, live chat spikes and the transcript.

Moment editor

Each candidate is checked on keyframes and the timestamped transcript, then cut so it opens fast and ends on the payoff.

Link clipping

Paste a stream link with a time and the Studio clips just that moment.

Editing

Streamer and game stacked

The webcam is found automatically and placed above the gameplay; a clip without the streamer's face in frame is flagged.

Captions and hooks

Word-timed captions from the speech, a short opening hook, and a highlight-first option that opens on the best second.

Safety checks

Burned-in captions from the source are hidden, nudity is detected and blurred, and clips that are not GTA are held back.

Publishing

Posting schedule

Approved Shorts are booked into fixed daily slots per channel, within each platform's posting limits.

TikTok

Posts through TikTok's Content Posting API after showing the account, the privacy choice, interaction settings and the commercial content disclosure.

Performance

View, like, comment and share counts of our own posts are read back so we can see which clips are working.
